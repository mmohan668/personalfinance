ABOUT EXPENSETRACK

Your family's finances, all in one place.

ExpenseTrack helps your family organize income, track expenses, monitor investments, and manage transfers — giving everyone a clearer picture of the family's financial life.

OUR PURPOSE

Make every transaction count.

Managing family finances shouldn't mean juggling multiple records or wondering where the money went. ExpenseTrack brings your family's financial transactions together so you can understand your money, make informed decisions, and plan ahead with confidence.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK

A complete view of your money

Keep track of the four essential types of family financial transactions.

Income

Record the money coming into your family, from salaries, business earnings, and other sources of income.

Expenses

Track everyday spending, household bills, shopping, education, and other family expenses.

Investments

Keep your investment transactions organized and maintain a clearer view of your family's financial goals.

Transfers

Record money moved between accounts and keep internal transfers visible in your transaction history.
